# Hokan

> Hokan, Inc. (株式会社hokan) is a Japanese InsurTech company that builds hokan®, a cloud-based customer and contract management (CRM) system for insurance agencies and brokerages. The platform digitizes proposal, activity, renewal, and accounting workflows for agents. Backed by 500 Global. No public developer portal, OpenAPI, or SDK is published; this is a company record in the API Evangelist network.
